Screening strategies were compared over 150 random subsampling rounds of the external validation set, with 100 compound combinations sampled per round. Within each subset, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, Extra Trees, Random Forest, and Decision Tree independently ranked combinations by their predicted probability of MIC < 0.5 mM and generated Top 20 candidate lists. The voting ensemble retained the intersection of these five lists (5/5 votes). The ALogP-only logistic regression baseline selected its Top 20 candidates by predicted probability, while random screening selected 20 combinations at random. Hit rate was calculated separately for each strategy and constituent model as the proportion of selected candidates with measured MIC < 0.5 mM, and averaged across rounds with nonempty candidate sets.
